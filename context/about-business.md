# About the Business

Source: `aios-intake.md` Q1, Q4, Q5; store details from the Holiday 2026 plan (project doc `claude/holiday-2026-plan.md`). Onboarded 2026-11-14.

**Offer.** Angel Coffee Company is an online coffee shop on Shopify (angelcoffeecompany.co). 74 products. Hero items: three $26.99 sample packs, holiday flavors (Pecan Pie, Turtle), and Wulan's Favorites. Shipping is free on every order.

**Who we serve.** People who love coffee and use it to connect with others and explore cultures through coffee. Ideally people who love to share their coffee. US-based for now, any region, no age limit. Everyone from connoisseurs to novices.

**Revenue model.** Direct online sales. Revenue lands and is tracked only in Shopify (Basic plan). 27 total orders to date; last order Oct 29, 2026.

**How customers find us.** Instagram (@angelcoffeecompany) and Facebook are the main channels. Word of mouth matters a lot. Past buyers (14) get emails through Shopify Email.

**Current campaign.** Holiday 2026: HOLIDAY10 (10% sitewide, Nov 14–30) and THANKYOU15 (15% for past buyers, Nov 14–Dec 14). Full plan: https://claude.ai/artifact/XBZs5VZ2tYtcFoHN4JYods
